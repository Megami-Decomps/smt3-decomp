# Matching notes: newbattle/nbE911

## Effect2 (newbattle/nbE911, 456 bytes)

94 of 114 words match at the exact 456-byte size, and the instruction multiset
is retail's. The dispatch is exact: the `e->data->form` load hoisted above the
tree, the `case 0x4B`/`case 0x53` arms cross-jumped onto one `nbCheckLoad` with
the `e->seqtime--` and `form = NULL` tail, the `case 0x8C` call to
`nbFreeEffectSlot`, the five packet calls guarded on `form`, and both
`(0x48 - t) << 7` and `(0x50 - t) << 7` divided by a local 12 with the `break
0, 7` guard.

The empty `case 0:` is load-bearing, not decoration: with only the three real
cases `balance_case_nodes` takes its `i == 3` shortcut and roots the tree at the
middle value, giving `beq 0x53` first. A fourth node moves the root to 0x4B and
reproduces retail's `beq 0x4B`, `slti 0x4C`, `beq 0x53`, `beq 0x8C` chain, so
retail's switch had a fourth label whose value is not recoverable from the code;
`case 0:` is a placeholder for it and changes no behaviour.

The 20 remaining words are the two colour-fade blocks, ten words each, the same
instructions with different register names: retail puts the quotient in `$3` and
walks the `work`/`prim` chain through `$5` twice, and the draft uses `$4` and
`$6`/`$5`; retail also fills the `beqz` delay slot with the `alpha << 24` shift
where the draft fills it with the mask's `lui`.

Ruled out:

- An if/else chain for the dispatch, which emits `bne` to the next test with the
  arms inline instead of retail's out-of-line arms: 46 of 114.
- Inverting the first test (`if (t != 0x4B) ... else`) to push the arm out of
  line, which loses a word and 99 of 113.
- Writing the colour store as `(masked) | (alpha << 24)` rather than
  `(alpha << 24) | (masked)`; the alpha-first order is what fixes the two
  global registers, four of the twenty words.
- Naming the chain in one, two, or no locals, and `u32` for `alpha`.
